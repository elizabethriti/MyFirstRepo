## Brand & Style

This design system channels an eccentric 1970s neo-diner aesthetic tailored for a vibrant, beloved neighborhood pizzeria. Drawing directly from the punchy visual personality of graphic diner ephemera and vintage menu layouts, it marries high-contrast tomato hues, gentle dough-and-mozzarella tints, and tactile charm with modern digital usability.

The atmosphere is unapologetically warm, appetizing, and cheerful. It avoids cold corporate cleanliness in favor of tactile pill-shapes, chunky display typography, vintage sign-painted flourishes, and bold structural borders. Designed for hungry patrons, neighborhood regulars, and food lovers seeking artisanal comfort, the visual voice radiates communal hospitality, cheeky warmth, and irresistible craveability.

## Colors

The color palette reflects classic artisanal pizzeria ingredients, reinterpreted through a bold 1970s print sensibility:

- **Primary (`#E02B20` - Sizzling Tomato Red):** The foundational brand color. Used for dominant action buttons, primary badges, high-impact headings, active tabs, and structural container borders.
- **Secondary (`#FDECEF` - Dough Blush):** A soft, creamy mozzarella-and-flour blush tone drawn from the reference card. Used for card backgrounds, banner fields, surface containers, and tinted chips, softening the intensity of the tomato red.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B26` - Roasted Crust Gold):** An oregano-and-olive oil gold accent reserved for special dietary tags, crust callouts, chef picks, and celebratory starburst badges.
- **Neutral (`#231512` - Charred Espresso Crust):** An intense, warm deep-roasted charcoal tone used for legible body copy, secondary subtitles, and crisp framing, ensuring superior contrast without feeling cold or clinical.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFDFB` - Semolina White):** An ultra-warm parchment base for page backgrounds that preserves high readability while keeping the palette rich and edible.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the quirky, expressive punch of Bricolage Grotesque with the warm, utilitarian legibility of Karla:

- **Bricolage Grotesque:** Applied to headlines, hero announcements, pizza names, and price callouts. Its chunky rhythm, unexpected ink traps, and energetic curves recall vintage 1970s diner menus and screen-printed posters.
- **Karla:** Drives menu ingredient breakdowns, order summaries, navigation links, and operational details. Its grotesque heritage maintains personality while ensuring immediate legibility even in dense digital cart flows.
- **Micro-Copy & Accents:** For secondary script sign-offs (akin to the "love, Lizzie" motif on the reference handout), an italicized flourish or custom badge styling can be paired with small-caps tracking in Karla.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy balances structured diner grid modularity with roomy, generous touch targets:

- **Grid Architecture:** A responsive 12-column fluid grid for desktop with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 40px (`2.5rem`) canvas margins. On mobile devices (below 768px), this collapses into a 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins.
- **Diner Banner Rhythm:** Structural horizontal bands—echoing the top and bottom red border dividers seen in the reference material—are used to slice sections, feature daily slice specials, and frame promo headers.
- **Menu Presentation:** Pizza item cards can be presented in pairs or trios on desktop and fluid 2-column or full-width vertical stacks on mobile, maintaining equal vertical gaps for price and modifier clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids realistic blur-heavy dropshadows, leaning instead on physical, graphic depth:

- **Tactile Stacking:** Surfaces utilize solid color layering—such as dough blush `#FDECEF` floating atop parchment white canvas `#FFFDFB`.
- **Crisp Outlines & Contrast Borders:** Elements gain definition from 1.5px to 2px solid perimeter strokes in primary tomato `#E02B20` or charred crust `#231512`.
- **Diner Hard Shadows:** For interactive buttons and floating cart widgets, visual lift is achieved through an offset, unblurred solid drop (e.g., `3px 3px 0px #231512`), creating a tactile, print-inspired press-button feel that compresses into flat alignment when active.

## Shapes

With a roundedness index of `3`, the shape language is defined by pill silhouettes, hyper-rounded squircle cards, and playful oval frames:

- **Oval Emblems:** Signature restaurant branding, slice badges, and combo tags employ prominent horizontal and vertical oval frames directly inspired by the reference marquee.
- **Pill Forms:** Buttons, dietary tags, category selectors, and quantity adjusters are rendered in full circular pills (`rounded-full`).
- **Cards & Image Frames:** Product image cards mimic the rounded pill squircle tiles found in the reference layout, applying large radii (`rounded-2xl` / `2rem`) to give every menu item an inviting, friendly form.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid tomato red `#E02B20` background, semolina white text, full pill curvature, with an optional 2px solid `#231512` offset drop shadow for added tactile feedback.
  - *Secondary / Outline:* Blush pink `#FDECEF` fill with a 2px `#E02B20` stroke and tomato-colored Karla bold typography.
- **Cards (Pizza & Menu Items):**
  - Features the smooth, soft pill-like frame of the reference card. Backgrounds use secondary `#FDECEF`.
  - Food imagery sits inside an arched or rounded squircle container at the top, followed by bold Bricolage Grotesque titles and charred crust `#231512` price points centered directly below.
- **Badges & Category Chips:**
  - Small pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`) in either olive gold `#D99B26` (for specialties) or dough blush `#FDECEF` with red borders (for dietary flags like Vegan or Gluten-Free).
- **Form Inputs & Modifier Selectors:**
  - Input fields and quantity pickers feature generous 1rem internal padding, 2px borders, and pill radii. Checkboxes and radio buttons appear as rounded circular or squoval stamps.
- **Banners & Dividers:**
  - Continuous horizontal ribbons with solid primary red dividers (`#E02B20`) containing centered sign-off messages, opening hours, or delivery status updates.
